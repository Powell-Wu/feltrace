Formation of nanosecond multibunch structure in thermionic electron beams and generation of multibunch x-ray free-electron laser pulses

中文导读

作者开发了一种利用宽带RF放大器和带高阻抗带状线电极的新型斩波腔的快速脉冲发生器，用于从SACLA X射线自由电子激光设施单晶CeB6热阴极发射的长脉冲束中可靠、精确地切出短束团。作者报告，利用这些装置成功在热阴极电子束中形成纳秒多束团结构，并在10 keV下产生总脉冲能量为1 mJ的双束团XFEL脉冲。作者称该结果将为XFEL光束提供更高强度、更高相干性和更高脉冲重复频率的新可能。

完整原文摘要

EN
The x-ray free-electron laser (XFEL) facility SACLA uses a thermionic pulsed electron gun and a beam chopper to produce a low-emittance, short-bunch beam. Recently, a new type of fast pulse generator, which utilized wideband rf amplifiers and a new chopper chamber with high-impedance strip-line electrodes, was developed to reliably and accurately cut out a short bunch from the long-pulsed beam emitted from a single-crystal CeB6 hot cathode. Using these apparatuses, we succeeded in forming nanosecond multibunch structure in the thermionic electron beam and generating a double-bunch XFEL pulse with a total pulse energy of 1 mJ at 10 keV. The results will provide new possibilities for XFEL beams: higher intensity, higher coherence, and higher pulse repetition rate.
