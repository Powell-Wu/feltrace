源语言
A very-high-frequency (VHF) electron gun operating at a resonant frequency of 216.667 MHz has been developed at Tsinghua University for the Shenzhen Superconducting Soft X-ray Free-Electron Laser (S3FEL\rm S^3FELS3FEL) facility. Building upon the SHINE gun design, the cavity profile was optimized to achieve a higher cathode electric-field gradient and a higher accelerating voltage at comparable input power. The revised cavity geometry also yields improved multipacting performance. In addition, thermal analysis was carried out to guide the water-cooling design, and enhanced cooling was implemented in the vicinity of the cathode. During high-power conditioning, 82 kW of continuous-wave radio-frequency power was successfully coupled into the gun, corresponding to a cathode gradient of 29.9 MV/m and an accelerating voltage of 856 kV. The gun voltage surpasses the previous world record for room-temperature VHF guns. The maximum dark current measured by the Faraday cup at the gun exit was only 6.8 nA.

中文导读 · 依据全文

如何在相近射频功率下提高连续波电子枪的加速电压，同时控制倍增和热失谐？清华大学作者团队以 SHINE/DALS 枪为基线，为 S³FEL 优化腔型、阴极塞及水冷结构。该 arXiv v1 预印本报告：在 82 kW 连续波射频测试中，用冷测品质因数和输入功率缩放设计结果，推得枪电压 856 kV、阴极梯度 29.9 MV/m；出口法拉第筒测得暗电流 6.8 nA。文中结合电磁、倍增和热分析解释设计取舍，并展示冷测、老炼及闭环稳定性结果。需区分模拟、缩放推算与直接测量；温度图和相邻正文还存在 75/82 kW 的功率标注差异。本文支持对枪体射频运行表现的判断，尚不能据此确认光电子束发射度或半导体阴极寿命。

相近射频功率下，提高加速电压仍需兼顾启动倍增

VHF（甚高频）光阴极枪以连续波射频场支持高重频电子源。本文面向 S³FEL 的枪体研制；论文介绍该设施设计束能为 2.5 GeV、束团重复频率为 1 MHz，这些是设施背景，不能当作本次枪体测试产生的束流指标。比较基线是作者此前的 SHINE/DALS 枪：216.667 MHz、75 kW 高功率测试中报告 780 kV 枪电压及 27 MV/m 阴极梯度。
